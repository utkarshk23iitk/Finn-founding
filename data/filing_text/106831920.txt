CHOLAMANDALAM INVESTMENT AND FINANCE COMPANY LIMITED (CIFCL)  
 
AUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER AND HALF YEAR ENDED  
30th SEPTEMBER 2026 
Key Financial Results (Q2 and H1 FY 26-27): 
• Total AUM at ₹ 2,68,009 Cr (Up by 25% YoY) 
• Net Income for the quarter was at ₹ 5,037 Cr (Up by 24% YoY) and ₹ 9,967 Cr 
for the half year ended (Up by 26% YoY) 
• PAT at ₹ 1,630 Cr for the quarter (Up by 41% YoY) and ₹ 3,284 Cr for the half 
year ended (Up by 43%) 
Chennai, October 28th, 2026: The Board of Directors of CIFCL today approved the audited 
financial results for the quarter and half ended 30th September 2026. 
 
Performance Highlights: 
 
 
• Aggregate disbursements in Q2 FY 27 were at ₹ 35,872 Cr and ₹ 65,484 in H1 FY 27 with 
the AUM of ₹ 2,68,009 Cr as of 30th September 2026 as compared to ₹ 2,14,906 Cr as of 
30th September 2025, registering a growth of 25% on Y-O-Y. 
 
• Vehicle Finance (VF) disbursements were at ₹ 17,801 Cr in Q2 FY 2 7 and ₹ 34,304 Cr in 
H1 FY 2 7, with the AUM of ₹ 1,29,787 Cr as of 30 th September 2026 as compared to ₹ 
1,07,568 Cr in the previous year, registering a growth of 21% Y-o-Y. 
 
• Loan Against Property (LAP) business disbursed ₹  6,438 Cr in Q2 FY 2 7 and ₹ 11,218 Cr 
in H1 FY 2 7, with the AUM of ₹ 57,447 Cr as of 30 th September 2026 as compared to ₹ 
46,302 Cr in the previous year, registering a growth of 24% Y-o-Y. 
 
• Home Loan business disbursed ₹ 2,426 Cr in Q2 FY 27, and ₹ 4,223 in H1 FY 27, with the 
AUM of ₹ 25,240 Cr as of 30th September 2026 as compared to ₹ 20,405 Cr in the previous 
year, registering a growth of 24% Y-o-Y.

• Consumer and Small Enterprise Loans (CSEL) disbursed ₹ 4,339 Cr in Q 2 FY 27, and ₹ 
7,747 Cr in H1 FY27, with the AUM of ₹ 17,216 Cr as of 30th September 2026 as compared 
to ₹ 13,952 Cr in the previous year, registering a growth of 23% Y-o-Y. 
 
• Secured Business and Personal Loan (SBPL) disbursed ₹ 454 Cr in Q2 FY 27, and ₹ 833 Cr 
in H1 FY27, with the AUM of ₹ 3,966 Cr as of 30th September 2026 as compared to ₹ 2,932 
Cr in the previous year, registering a growth of 35% Y-o-Y. 
 
• Gold Loans (GL) business disbursed ₹ 1,960 Cr in Q2 FY27 and ₹ 2,714 Cr in H1 FY27, with 
the AUM of ₹ 3,446 Cr as of 30th September 2026 and continued to scale steadily. We are 
currently operating through 308 dedicated branches. 
 
• PBT Growth was 40% in Q2 and 43% in H1 of FY 27. 
 
• PBT-ROA for Q2 is at 3.4% and 3.5% in H1 FY 27. 
 
• ROE for Q2 FY 27 was at 19.43%. and 20.26% in the H1 FY 27. 
 
• The Company continues to hold a strong liquidity position with ₹ 21,352 Cr as cash balance 
as at end of Sep'2026 (including HQLA of ₹ 7,745.42 Cr invested in GSEC / SDL & Strips 
shown under investments held in compliance with LCR requirements), with a total liquidity 
position of ₹ 21,974 Cr (including undrawn sanctioned lines). The ALM is comfortable with 
no negative cumulative mismatches across all time buckets. 
 
• Consolidated Profit Before Tax (PBT) for Q2 FY 27 was at ₹ 2,187 Cr as against ₹ 1,565 Cr 
in Q2 FY 26 registering a growth of 40% and for H1 FY 27 was at ₹ 4,409 Cr as against ₹ 
3,096 Cr in H1 FY 26 registering a growth of 42%. 
 
Asset Quality: 
Stage 3 levels representing 90+ dues were at 3.25% as of September 26 as against 3.29% at 
the end of June 26. GNPA % as per RBI norms  was at 4.44% as of September 26 as against 
4.50% in June 26. NNPA as per RBI norms was at 2.92% as of September 26 as against 2.95% 
in June 26.

Capital Adequacy: 
 
The Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of the company as o f 30th September 2026, was at 20.62% 
as against the regulatory requirement of 15%. Tier-I Capital was at 1 6.02% (Common Equity 
Tier-I Capital at 1 4.88% as against a regulatory minimum of 9%)  and Tier-II Capital was at 
4.60%. As on Sep-26 the conversion of CCDs into equity shares was completed.
