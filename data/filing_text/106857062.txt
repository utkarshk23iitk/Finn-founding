Good afternoon, everyone. I will first share an update on the ESG and then on each of our business segments, followed by an update 
on our strategic priorities. Let me start with ESG. Our ESG progress is closely aligned with our business strategy and the choices we 
make to build a more sustainable, efficient, and future -ready enterprise. We are using technology and digital innovation to improve 
the way we operate , reduce resource intensity, and drive greater efficiency across the business.  A key priority in this journey is to 
increase the share of energy from alternate and renewable sources.  This is reflected in our continued expansion of solar -powered 
deployment and reduction in diesel consumption across our network infrastructure.  Over the last two years, we  have accelerated 
solarization across our network, which is now operational on about 50,000 sites. 
 
Let me have a quick run-through on each of our businesses. Mobile, in Q2, we added 0.7 million revenue-earning customers and 2.9 
million smartphone data customers during the quarter. Our post-paid segment delivered yet another quarter of solid performance, with 
the highest ever net ads of 1.2 million.  Post-paid customers now account for 8.3% of our total customer base, and over the past 4 
quarters they have accounted for nearly 30% of all customer net adds. ARPU for the quarter came in at Rs.271, up from 264, benefited

In homes, we delivered net adds of 670,000. Its momentum is building up from the last quarter. We are seeing strong and consistent 
traction on the connected homes, driven by our convergence agenda.  
 
Digital TV, we lost over 4 lakh customers during the quarter led by the DTH business , while our IPTV adoption continues to remain 
strong. Our IPTV take rates continue to deliver on our convergence agenda and you will hear more about it in the coming quarters.  
 
Airtel business revenues came in at about Rs.5,800 Crores, growing 3.8% sequentially and 11.4% over last year same quarter. During 
the quarter, we secured large deal wins across both Core Connectivity and the digital portfolio. We continue to see strong funnel and 
order bookings in the business.  
 
Our digital portfolio delivered another quarter of solid performance, with revenue growth of about 4% sequentially.  We are seeing 
strong traction across our portfolio including cloud, cybersecurity, IoT, digital platforms, and CPaaS and we saw some notable deal 
wins during the quarter. 
 
Let me now provide an update on our strategic pillars. First, winning quality customers. Let me start from homes. We continue to see

digital content consumption continue to fuel the need for high-speed broadband connectivity. As I mentioned earlier, demand remains 
concentrated in the top 1000 cities, and we have sharpened our acquisition strategy to scale our presence in these towns. Targeting 
customer cohorts with stronger engagement and retention. Our strategy is centered on building a high-quality customer franchise by 
delivering a superior connectivity, differentiated offerings, and a seamless service proposition.  We are investing in fiber home  pass

touchpoints into a single view.  At the core is our Converge Data Engine, which combines data, AI, and analytics to bring real -time 
context to every interaction, anticipating customer needs and delivering a personalized experience at scale.  The result is a simpler, 
more relevant interaction, and a deeper relationship with our customers. 
 
The third pillar of our strategy is to build and leverage our digital capabilities. AI is increasingly woven into the fabric of our business. 
Over the past two quarters, we have laid the foundation to embed AI at the heart of Airtel’s digital platforms. We have built a homegrown 
agent platform called Agent Bridge and added GPU capacity across our data centers.  This is enabling us to activate AI use cases 
much faster across both customer -facing and internal journeys.  In parallel, we have run several AI experiments to reimagine our 
operating models with an intent to redesign how work gets done at Airtel. Going forward, our focus is on scaling the use cases already 
creating strong value. 
 
Let me share a few highlights. For customers, our real-time, context-led decision engine has now generated over 10 billion next best 
actions and is scaled across key inventories across our channels.  We use individual -level context to deliver more than a billion

and nearly 5 billion messages, and blocked over 1.7 million fraudulent links. For networks, AI is helping us lower our fuel and power 
wastage, which reduces operating costs while also improving customer experience.  Our AI-enabled RAM dynamically orchestrated 
over 14 million sleep hours each day across our network sites during Q2, reducing energy consumption and generating some cost 
savings. We see substantial headroom to further optimize our energy costs as these capabilities scale across the network. The third 
area is internal functions  where AI tools are now changing the way our internal processes are.  Call center bots were the early use 
case we adopted. Now, AI-led processes are changing across areas like quarterly financial book closure, recruitment, mass interview 
screening of candidates, and our video KYCs for our customer acquisition. As we scale AI across Airtel, we are also remaining focused 
on clear guardrails around customer safety, privacy, and data sovereignty. 
 
The fourth pillar of our strategy is War on Waste. Cost discipline remains core to how we operate and as cost pressures build, funding 
the growth will depend on how much waste we take out of the system.  The War on Waste initiative continues. We are also looking 
beyond efficiency towards innovation that changes the cost equation itself , in areas like energy  and in virtualizing set -up boxes for 
IPTV, for example. These initiatives are helping us mitigate inflationary pressures arising from ongoing geopolitical headwinds.
