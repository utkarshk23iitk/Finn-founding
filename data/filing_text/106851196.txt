ANALYSIS OF RESULTS 
QUARTER & SIX MONTHS ENDED 30th September 2026 
Voltas Delivers Steady Q2 FY27 Performance, on the back of market leadership and Execution 
Excellence 
Voltas continued to strengthen its competitive position during Q2 FY27, supported by market leadership 
in Room Air Conditioners, continued expansion of its Home Appliances business and disciplined 
execution across its Projects and Engineering businesses. 
The quarter was characterised by moderate global economic growth, continuing geopolitical 
uncertainties, commodity inflation and currency volatility. These developments created cost pressures 
across manufacturing and supply chains. Against this backdrop, the Indian consumer durables industry 
benefited from relatively stable domestic demand, with an extended summer providing additional 
support to the cooling category during a traditionally lean quarter. 
Voltas recorded 32% volume growth in its Room Air Conditioner business , materially outperforming 
the broader industry. The Company further consolidated its position as India's No. 1 Room Air 
Conditioner brand, achieving a  market share of 17.4% for the year to date ended September 2026 , 
representing an increase of approximately 1.5 percentage points over the previous year. Its lead over 
the nearest competitor stood at 4.2%. 
The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29 % revenue growth, accompanied by improved 
profitability despite commodity and currency related pressures. Voltbek achieved its highest quarterly 
sales in both value and volume, while the Projects business maintained a carry forward order book 
exceeding ₹6,800 crore. Engineering Products and Services recorded high double digit revenue growth, 
reflecting the contribution of the Company's diversified business portfolio. 
The performance reflects Voltas' continued focus on translating market leadership into business growth, 
improving operating efficiencies and strengthening the foundations for sustainable profitability. 
Financial Performance Overview 
For the quarter ended 30th September 2026, Voltas recorded: 
• Consolidated Total Income of ₹ 2,746 crores, compared to ₹ 2,412 crores in Q2 FY26. 
• Profit Before Tax of ₹ 83 crores versus ₹ 54 crores in Q2 FY26. 
• Net Profit of ₹ 48 crores compared to ₹ 32 crores last year. 
The Company's operating priorities continued to centre on market competitiveness, profitable growth, 
working capital discipline and investment in capabilities that support its long-term strategic objectives. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Segment A – Unitary Cooling Products (UCP) 
Room Air Conditioners: Consolidating Market Leadership 
The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29% revenue growth during Q2 FY27, driven primarily 
by the Room Air Conditioner business, which achieved  32% volume growth during the quarter. 
The performance reflects Voltas' ability to capture market opportunities through its established brand 
franchise, comprehensive product portfolio, distribution strength and effective execution across 
channels and geographies. 
Voltas maintained its position as India's No. 1 Room Air Conditioning brand, achieving a 17.4% market 
share for the year to date ended September 2026 , an improvement of approximately 1.5 percentage 
points over the corresponding period of the previous year. The Company maintained a  4.2 percentage 
point lead over its nearest competitor, reinforcing its competitive position in the Indian RAC market. 
The combination of value and volume growth reflects the strength of consumer demand for Voltas 
products, supported by improved product positioning, portfolio competitiveness and sustained 
investments in distribution and consumer engagement. 
The Company continued to respond to evolving consumer preferences through a sharper product 
portfolio across capacities, energy efficiency ratings, technologies and price points. Greater emphasis 
on product differentiation, energy efficiency and consumer r elevant features supported the 
competitiveness of the portfolio. 
Distribution expansion remained a key priority, particularly in emerging consumption centres and 
markets where RAC penetration continues to remain relatively low. The Company continued to 
strengthen its channel partnerships, improve product availability an d expand market coverage to 
capture opportunities arising from increasing adoption of air conditioning across India. 
Voltas remains focused on building upon its established leadership through sustained investments in 
product innovation, brand development, distribution reach and customer experience. 
Commercial Air Conditioning and Complementary Cooling Businesses 
The Commercial Air Conditioning business continued to record stable growth, supported by demand 
across commercial real estate, healthcare, hospitality, retail, manufacturing and data centres.  The 
business benefits from a diversified customer base and  lower seasonal dependence compared with 
Room Air Conditioners, providing greater balance to the overall cooling portfolio. 
 
 
 
Continued investments in infrastructure and institutional development, alongside demand for energy 
efficient and technologically advanced cooling solutions, provide opportunities for further expansion. 
Home Comfort products , comprising Air Coolers, Water Heaters and Fans, recorded healthy growth 
during the quarter, while Commercial Refrigeration remained relatively flat, reflecting softer demand. 
The Company continues to focus on expanding key account relationships, strengthening institutional 
sales, improving channel penetration and introducing relevant product innovations to support growth 
across these businesses. 
Together, these categories complement the RAC business and contribute to the breadth, diversification 
and resilience of the Unitary Cooling Products portfolio. 
Recent global developments have contributed to higher input costs and currency volatility, creating 
challenges for the broader consumer durables industry.  Voltas continued to address these pressures 
through calibrated pricing actions and structural cost optimisation initiatives undertaken since FY26. 
These include strategic sourcing, greater localisation, product redesign, manufacturing productivity 
improvements and tighter operating cost management. 
The benefits of these initiatives, together with revenue growth and operating efficiencies, contributed 
to improved segment profitability compared with the corresponding period of the previous year.  The 
Company's focus remains on strengthening cost competitiveness while preserving its market position, 
investing in innovation and improving absolute profitability. 
With an established brand franchise, industry leading market position, healthy inventory levels and an 
expanding distribution network, Voltas remains focused on sustaining market share gains and 
translating business growth into improved profitability. 
Strategic Manufacturing and Localisation Initiatives 
Voltas continues to strengthen its manufacturing and supply chain capabilities through localisation, 
strategic sourcing, product engineering and operational efficiency initiatives. 
Greater localisation of critical components, including opportunities relating to compressor sourcing and 
manufacturing, remains an important strategic priority for improving supply chain resilience, reducing 
import dependence and enhancing long term cost competitiveness. 
The Company continues to evaluate opportunities for strengthening its manufacturing ecosystem and 
developing partnerships that can support product innovation, technology access and future growth.  
These initiatives form part of the broader strategy to build a more competitive, efficient and resilient 
operating model while supporting sustainable profitability. 
 
 
 
 
Voltbek Home Appliances: Scaling Growth and Market Presence 
Voltbek continued its expansion during Q2 FY27, recording its highest Q2  sales in both value and 
volume, supported by an expanding product portfolio, increasing consumer acceptance and deeper 
distribution penetration. 
The business continued to outperform the broader home appliances market and strengthen its position 
across key product categories.  For the year to date ended September 2026, Voltbek achieved market 
shares of approximately  8.8% in Washing Machines and 7. 0% in Refrigerators. The business also 
maintained its No. 2 position in the Semi Automatic Washing Machine category, with a market share of 
approximately 14.9%. 
These gains reflect continued progress in building brand consideration, expanding retail presence and 
expanding product portfolio. Voltbek continued to strengthen its offerings across higher value segments 
through refreshed product lines, improved product architecture, higher capacity models and sharper 
consumer segmentation. 
The business also launched its new  campaign, "Voltas Beko: Sense Toh Banta Hai" , supported by 
refreshed product offerings and enhanced consumer engagement aimed at strengthening brand 
awareness and market penetration. 
Distribution expansion, retail visibility and product availability remain key priorities as Voltbek works 
towards increasing its presence across established and emerging consumption markets.  In parallel, 
sourcing efficiencies and manufacturing productivity initiatives are expected to support improved cost 
competitiveness and profitability as the business continues to scale. 
With its expanding market presence, increasing product relevance and sustained investments in brand 
building, Voltbek is emerging as an important growth pillar for Voltas. The business continues to 
enhance Voltas’ channel relevance complementing the Company's established leadership in cooling 
products while strengthening its position as an all season, full stack consumer durables player. 
Segment B: Electro-Mechanical Projects and Services (EMPS) 
The Electro-Mechanical Projects and Services business continued to provide diversification and revenue 
visibility to the Voltas portfolio, supported by its established engineering capabilities, project execution 
expertise and presence across domestic and international markets. 
  
 
 
 
 
Domestic Projects 
The Domestic Projects business maintained order momentum during Q2 FY27, securing projects across 
industrial infrastructure, electronics manufacturing, metro and tunnel projects, and data centres.  The 
business continued to prioritise selective order booking, focusing on projects that align with its 
engineering capabilities, execution strengths and profitability objectives. 
Greater emphasis on project selection, contractual discipline, working capital management and 
execution efficiency remains central to improving the quality of the order book and supporting 
sustainable profitability. 
The Company continues to pursue opportunities in infrastructure and industrial development while 
maintaining a disciplined approach to risk and capital deployment. 
International Projects 
The International Projects business continued to focus on operational stability, disciplined execution 
and prudent risk management against the backdrop of geopolitical uncertainties in the Middle East.  
During Q1 FY27, Sidra Bank Guarantees aggregating QAR 167 million, equivalent to approximately ₹438 
crore, were cancelled pursuant to a favourable court award. 
The subsequent appeal filed by the main contractor before the Court of Cassation was dismissed, 
upholding the earlier judgment and bringing further clarity to the matter. The resolution represents an 
important development in reducing contingent exposure and strengthening the risk profile of the 
International Projects business. 
The Company continued to maintain business continuity across key projects and customer relationships, 
while prioritising tighter project controls, selective bidding and working capital discipline.  As of 30 th  
September 2026, the total order pad f o r Se gm e n t  B  s t o o d  a t  o ve r ₹ 6, 800 crore, providing revenue 
visibility across Domestic and International Projects.  The business remains focused on converting its 
order book through disciplined execution, improving cash flow and maintaining a selective approach to 
new opportunities. 
Segment C: Engineering Products and Services 
The Engineering Products and Services segment delivered  robust revenue growth during Q2 FY27 , 
supported by performance across Mining and Construction Equipment and Textile Machinery.  The 
segment continues to provide diversification to the Voltas portfolio through its presence across 
industrial equipment, aftermarket services and long standing customer relationships. 
Its relatively lower seasonal dependence and growing contribution from service businesses provide 
additional balance to the Company's earnings profile. 
 
 
 
Mining and Construction Equipment 
The Mining and Construction Equipment business recorded revenue growth, led by demand for crushing 
and screening equipment, continued execution of Operations and Maintenance contracts and stable 
performance from African operations. 
Aftermarket services and recurring maintenance activities continued to contribute to the business, 
supporting customer engagement and improving the quality of revenue. The business remains focused 
on expanding equipment opportunities, strengthening service capabilities and increasing the 
contribution from aftermarket and recurring service streams. 
Textile Machinery 
The Textile Machinery business continued to record strong  growth despite challenges arising from 
geopolitical uncertainties and international trade developments. 
Growth was supported by spinning and post spinning solutions, after sales services, spinning accessories 
and customer support offerings.  The division continued to leverage its established customer 
relationships and technical capabilities to strengthen its market presence and expand opportunities 
across products and services. 
The Engineering Products and Services segment remains focused on expanding its customer base, 
improving operational efficiencies and enhancing the contribution of services and aftermarket 
businesses to support sustainable growth and profitability. 
Balance Sheet, Liquidity and Working Capital 
Voltas maintained a healthy liquidity position and a disciplined approach to working capital 
management during the quarter. The Company continued to focus on cash flow generation, inventory 
management, receivables and prudent capital allocation across its businesses. 
Working capital discipline and operational efficiencies remain important priorities, particularly in the 
Projects business, where selective order booking and execution controls support improved cash flow 
management. 
The Company's financial position continues to support investments in brand building, product 
innovation, distribution expansion, manufacturing capabilities and strategic growth initiatives.  Voltas 
remains focused on balancing investments for future growth with financial discipline and sustainable 
returns. 
  
 
 
 
 
Outlook and Strategic Direction 
Voltas enters the festive season of FY27 with continued momentum across its businesses, supported by 
its established market leadership in Room Air Conditioners, the expanding scale of Voltbek and a 
diversified portfolio of Projects and Engineering businesses. 
The Company's strategic priorities remain centred on sustaining market leadership, improving absolute 
profitability, strengthening cost competitiveness and building capabilities for long term growth. 
In Room Air Conditioners, Voltas will continue to focus on consolidating and extending its market 
leadership through product innovation, sharper portfolio management, sustained brand investments 
and deeper distribution penetration. 
The Company will also maintain its focus on premiumisation, energy efficient products and technology 
led differentiation, while addressing commodity and currency related cost pressures through calibrated 
pricing and structural cost optimisation initiatives. 
Commercial Air Conditioning, Commercial Refrigeration and Home Comfort products will continue to 
complement the RAC business and contribute to the diversification of the cooling portfolio. 
Voltbek remains focused on accelerating scale, strengthening its position across Washing Machines and 
Refrigerators and improving profitability through new line of Frost Free Refrigerators and Fully 
Automatic Washing Machines and distribution expansion. 
In the Projects business, the emphasis will remain on selective and value accretive order booking, 
disciplined execution, working capital efficiency and sustainable profitability. 
Engineering Products and Services will continue to pursue growth opportunities through its diversified 
product portfolio, established customer relationships and expanding aftermarket and service 
capabilities. 
Across the organisation, Voltas will continue to invest in product innovation, manufacturing readiness, 
localisation, supply chain efficiencies, customer experience and operational excellence. 
With its leadership in cooling, expanding consumer durables portfolio, established engineering 
capabilities and disciplined approach to capital allocation, Voltas remains focused on translating 
market opportunities into sustainable growth and long term stakeholder value.
