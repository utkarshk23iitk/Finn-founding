Rationale and key rating drivers 
Reaffirmation of ratings to bank facilities of Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (M&M) continues to factor in its strong market position, 
with leadership in the domestic tractor industry, the sub -3.5 tonne light commercial vehicle (LCV) segment, the sport s utility 
vehicle (SUV) segment by revenue market share, and the electric three-wheeler segment. Ratings further derive strength from 
M&M’s diversified business profile, robust financial risk profile characterised by a strong capital structure and healthy deb t 
coverage metrics, and substantial financial flexibility supported by sizeable cash and liquid investments. Ratings also take 
cognisance of the company’s investments in certain listed group entities, where the market value of investments exceeds their 
carrying value, providing additional financial flexibility. These strengths largely offset risks arising from the inherent cyclicality of 
the automobile industry, increasing competitive intensity in the utility vehicle (UV) segment, and exposure associated with 
investments in subsidiaries and joint ventures (JVs). Ratings continue to derive comfort from the management’s prudent capital 
allocation strategy across group entities. CARE Ratings Limited (CareEdge Ratings) expects M&M to sustain its growth momentum, 
supported by its strong market position, diversified revenue streams, and ongoing focus on operational efficiencies. However, 
profitability will remain susceptible to movements in commodity prices and competitive pressures across the automotive and farm 
equipment segments.

Analytical approach: CareEdge Ratings has considered the consolidated financial profile of M&M, excluding its non-banking 
financial company (NBFC) subsidiary, Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited (MMFSL ; rated ‘CARE AAA; Stable/CARE 
A1+’), and its subsidiaries. However, the analysis factors in the likelihood of continued financial support from M&M to MMFSL, 
considering the strategic importance of the financial services business to the group. Subsidiaries consolidated are listed under 
Annexure-5. 
 
Outlook: Stable 
The Stable outlook reflects CareEdge Ratings’ expectation that M&M will continue to maintain its strong business risk profile , 
supported by its leadership position across key automotive and farm equipment segments, and a strong financial risk profile 
characterised by healthy cash accruals, strong liquidity , and substantial financial flexibility. The outlook also factors in the 
company's ability to sustain growth and maintain comfortable credit metrics despite industry cyclicality and competitive intensity.

Strong market position across key business segments, supporting sustained growth in scale and profitability 
M&M’s consolidated total operating income (excluding MMFSL) increased by ~26% to ₹176,440 crore in FY26 from ₹140,579 
crore in FY25 and ₹122,753 crore in FY24. Growth was primarily driven by sustained demand for the company’s SUVs, the first 
full year of commercialisation of its electric SUVs, including the XEV 9e and BE 6, and healthy traction across the three-wheeler 
business and M&M’s sustained leadership in the domestic tractor segment . Consolidated automotive and farm sectors volumes 
grew by ~20% year-on-year. 
 
Profit before interest, lease rentals, depreciation, and taxation (PBILDT) increased by ~24% to ₹23,240 crore in FY26, while the 
PBILDT margin remained broadly stable at 13.17% compared to 13.34% in FY25. Benefits from operating leverage, benign 
commodity prices and cost -rationalisation initiatives were partly offset by weaker profitability in the farm equipment segment, 
particularly due to losses in certain international operations. The management has undertaken portfolio-rationalisation measures, 
including exiting or restructuring select loss -making overseas businesses, which are expected to support profitability over the 
medium term. 
 
The growth momentum continued in Q1FY27, with total operating income and PBILDT increasing by 29.4% and 32.3% year-on-
year, respectively, and PBILDT margin remaining stable at 13.11%. The auto and farm segments are expected to sustain healthy 
growth in FY27, supported by a strong product pipeline, capacity additions, healthy demand for the company’s recently launched 
models, and continued market leadership across key segments. 
 
Robust financial risk profile supported by strong liquidity and net cash position 
M&M’s automobile business has robust capital structure and debt coverage indicators marked by overall gearing of 0. 09x (PY: 
0.11x) as on March 31, 2026, owing to repayment of term loan and reduction in stake of loss-making companies as a part of the 
capital allocation strategy. This combined with cash and liquid investment to the tune of ₹39,364 crore as at end-FY26 has led to 
continued net-debt-free status. M&M’s healthy internal accruals are expected to adequately fund the planned capacity expansion 
across its automotive, farm and electric vehicle businesses, including the Chakan and ₹15,000 -crore Nagpur projects, as well as 
investments in its subsidiaries/group companies. CareEdge Ratings believes that despite the planned capex, M&M’s financial risk 
profile would continue to remain robust and net debt free over the medium term. 
 
Highly experienced promoters and management 
M&M is the flagship company of the Mahindra Group, a diversified conglomerate with an operating track record of over eight 
decades and presence across multiple sectors through its subsidiaries, associates and joint ventures. The group has a presenc e 
across over 20 industries, including automotive, farm equipment, financial services, technology, logistics, hospitality, real estate, 
renewable energy, defence , aerospace etc. M&M benefits from an experienced promoter & management team, established 
governance framework and demonstrated execution capabilities, reflected in its leadership position across key automotive and 
farm equipment segments.  The company has maintained a prudent approach towards capital allocation and risk management 
while ensuring operational oversight across its major subsidiaries and businesses.

Liquidity: Strong 
M&M continues to maintain a strong liquidity position, with cash and cash equivalents of ₹ 39,364 crore as on March 31, 202 6, 
and unutilised fund -based bank lines of ~₹438 crore as on Ju ly 31, 202 6, on a standalone basis. The company also holds 
substantial investments in listed group companies with significant market value, providing additional financial flexibility. CareEdge 
Ratings expects the company's liquidity profile to remain strong despite the planned capex and investment outlay, which is 
expected to be funded largely through internal accruals. M&M also enjoys strong financial flexibility, supported by easy access to 
capital markets and sizeable investments in listed group companies. 
 
Environment, social, and governance (ESG) risks 
 
Environment 
M&M follows the Mahindra Group’s  “Planet Positive” framework, encompassing the greening of operations, industrial 
decarbonisation, climate adaptation and just transition, and ESG leadership. The company remains committed to achieving carbon 
neutrality for Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 2040 or earlier. Its science-based targets envisage reducing Scope 1 and Scope 
2 emission intensity by 47% per equivalent product unit and Scope 3 emission intensity by 30% per sold product unit by 2033, 
compared to the 2018 baseline. 
 
In FY26, M&M’s renewable electricity share stood at 37%. Renewable electricity generation increased by 7% year-on-year, while 
energy intensity per ₹ crore of revenue improved to 22.63 GJ in FY26 from 24.72 GJ in FY25. The company continues to pursue 
increased renewable-energy adoption, energy efficiency, water positivity, zero waste to landfill and greater material circularity 
across its operations. 
 
The company is also expanding its green portfolio through electric mobility, renewable energy, green logistics, sustainable 
buildings, vehicle recycling and climate -smart agricultural solutions. M&M retained leadership in electric three -wheelers with a 
40.0% market share in FY26 and emerged as the leading domestic electric-SUV player by revenue market share, demonstrating 
the commercial opportunity arising from the transition towards cleaner mobility. It is also working with suppliers to improve  
environmental practices and develop a net-zero supply chain; sustainability-awareness programmes have cumulatively covered 
72.4% of suppliers by procurement value since FY24. 
 
Social 
M&M’s CSR interventions focus on girls’ education, women’s skill development and livelihood creation, maternal and newborn 
healthcare, water conservation, sustainable agriculture and community resilience. The company incurred CSR expenditure of 
~₹220 crore in FY26, while aggregate CSR investment across the Mahindra Group, including subsidiaries, associates and joint 
ventures, stood at ₹392 crore. M&M also supported technical infrastructure and capacity building across 70 Industrial Trainin g 
Institutes to promote future-ready skills.
