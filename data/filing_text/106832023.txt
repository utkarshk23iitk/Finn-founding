Sector outlook – Vehicle Finance business (1/2)
▪ The Heavy Commercial Vehicle (HCV) segment recorded robust growth of 40% in Q2 and 27% in H1 
FY’27, achieving its highest-ever H1 sales. Industry demand was supported by strong freight movement, 
infrastructure-led transportation, increased movement of cement and steel, mining activity, and a 
revival in fleet replacement demand. Growth is expected to be steady in the second half. 
▪ The Light Commercial Vehicle (LCV) segment recorded robust growth of 25% in Q2 and 22% in H1 FY’27, 
achieving its highest-ever H1 sales. Growth was driven by strong last-mile logistics, expanding e-
commerce and quick-commerce deliveries, improving freight movement, and fleet replacement 
demand. There is a positive outlook for demand going forward.
▪ The Small commercial vehicle (SCV) segment delivered a growth of 26% in Q2 & 28% in H1 FY’27. The 
segment is showing steady improvement, and the positive momentum is expected to continue in the 
coming months. 
▪ The Passenger Vehicle (Car & MUV) segment registered robust growth of 31% in Q2 and 28% in H1 
FY’27, achieving its best-ever H1 performance. Growth was driven by improved vehicle affordability 
following GST 2.0, strong demand for SUVs and MUVs and new model launches. Festive demand will 
have a positive impact in this quarter.
▪ The Two-wheeler industry recorded a growth of 15% in Q2 & 18% in H1 FY’27, recording its highest ever 
in H1 sale. The two-wheeler industry is expected to sustain positive momentum, supported by festive 
and rural demand in the coming quarters. 28
Sector outlook – Vehicle Finance business (2/2)
Chola remains focused on capitalising on industry opportunities across key product segments, supported 
by underlying economic activity and end-use demand. The Company will pursue calibrated portfolio 
expansion, prioritising productive asset financing, customer cash flow fundamentals and disciplined credit 
standards. Emphasis will remain on maintaining asset quality, improving risk-adjusted returns, 
strengthening market presence and enhancing operational efficiency through robust collections and 
prudent portfolio management.
Cholas Position:
▪ The used vehicle market is expected to sustain its growth momentum, driven by increasing finance 
penetration, improved vehicle availability and the gradual formalisation of the industry. The outlook 
remains favourable, with more headroom for growth.
▪ The Construction Equipment (CE) segment recorded robust growth of 23% in Q2 and 16% in H1 FY’27, 
achieving its highest-ever H1 sales. Growth was supported by sustained infrastructure development, 
government capital expenditure, and healthy activity across road construction, mining and other 
infrastructure projects. Steady growth is expected in the coming quarters.
▪ The tractor industry recorded a 3% decline in Q2 FY’27, following strong growth in the preceding 
period, while H1 FY’27 sales grew by 8% year-on-year, achieving the highest-ever half-yearly sales in 
recent years. The Q2 moderation reflects a high base, uneven monsoon conditions and lower Kharif 
acreage. Growth in the second half depends on post-harvest cash flows, rural income trends and the 
impact of rainfall on agricultural output.
