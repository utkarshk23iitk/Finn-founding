L&T Wins Orders (Significant*) for Residential, 
Commercial Buildings & Factories Business 
 
Mumbai, November 02, 2026: The Residential, Commercial Buildings & Factories (RCF) 
business vertical of L&T has secured multiple orders from a prestigious client for the 
construction of two residential developments in Mumbai and Bengaluru, and a commercial 
development in Hyderabad.  
 
In Mumbai, the business has secured an order for the construction of nine high- rise 
residential towers, comprising one basement and 50 floors each. In Bengaluru, it has won 
an order for the construction of nine residential towers with three basements and 25 floors 
each. 
 
The commercial development  in Hyderabad entails the construction of a premium 
commercial tower comprising five basements and 22 floors.  The business has also secured 
add-on orders for some of its ongoing projects.  
 
The RCF business vertical of L&T possesses strong domain knowledge, proven expertise and 
extensive experience in delivering EPC solutions across segments such as, residential 
buildings, commercial spaces, semiconductor facilities and factories, including automobiles 
plants, proving grounds,and other complex industrial structures.
