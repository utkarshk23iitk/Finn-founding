on-year revenue growth  in the last 16 quarters, reflecting the strength of 
our brands, customer relationships and market positioning.  While 
profitability was impacted by elevated raw material  cost, disciplined cost 
management and operating efficiencies helped partially offset the 
inflationary pressures.

continue to strengthen our  ESG framework and are encouraged by the 
recognition received from leading ESG rating agencies. Operationally, our 
AP plant and our Kerala plant achieved Gold Level Zero Waste to Landfill 
Certification, further supporting our 2030 sustainability ambitions. 
 
Coming back to the results, let me briefly touch upon India operations to 
start with. India operations revenue increased by nearly 26% year-on-year 
to INR 59.2 billion, representing an acceleration over the growth delivered 
in the first quarter. The performance was  broad-based across both 
Replacement and OEM channels with volume growth and pricing 
contributing almost equally to the top-line expansion. 
 
Profitability remained affected by the sharp escalation in raw material cost. 
India EBITDA stood at INR 6.6 billion with a margin of 11.1% compared to 
15.3% in the corresponding period last year. However, I believe the more 
important takeaway from the first half year is the company's response to 
this inflation cycle. Since April 2026, raw material costs have increased by 
approximately 25%, making this one of the steepest and fastest cost 
inflation episodes we have experienced in many years. 
 
In response, Apollo has implemented pricing actions of up to 15% in the 
Replacement segment alongside meaningful price increases in the OEM 
business. While raw material prices will eventually stabilize and may even 
soften over a period of time, our objective is  to ensure that the enhanced 
pricing discipline demonstrated during this cycle  becomes a permanent 
feature of the business. We strongly believe that this will contribute to a 
fundamentally stronger, more resilient and more profitable business model 
over the long term. 
 
Looking ahead, we expect demand conditions in India to remain supportive. 
While reported volume growth may moderate over the coming quarters due 
to higher base, revenue momentum should continue to benefit from the 
pricing actions implemented since April.  
 
On margins, although the direction of recovery is clear, the price timing 
actually will depend on the trajectory of commodity markets and geopolitical 
developments. Our focus will remain on balancing growth with profitability 
while maintaining disciplined execution. 
 
Turning to Europe, the operating environment remained challenging during 
the quarter. Revenue  declined by 12% year -on-year to EUR 157 million, 
primarily reflecting weakness in the truck and agriculture segments, which 
was impacted by temporary supply disruptions as a fallout of our Enschede 
plant closure.  
 
But despite these challenges, there were encouraging signs beneath the 
surface. Our PCR business  continued to gain market share despite 
subdued demand  conditions, reinforcing the strength of  a brand and 
product proposition.  
 
EBITDA margins for the European operations for the quarter stood at 
10.3%. While our margin recovery trajectory in Europe has  taken longer 
than initially anticipated, that has been due  to the combination of market 
softness and transition -related challenges, but our strategic rationale 
remains unchanged. The actions we are undertaking today are intended to 
create a structurally more competitive  and financially stronger European 
business.  
 
Our immediate priorities remain clear, completing the manufacturing 
transition, stabilizing the supply chain and enhancing the cost 
competitiveness. Over the medium term, we remain committed to building 
a European business capable of delivering high teen operating margins. 
 
Moving on to the balance sheet, as we communicated previously, w e 
continue to invest behind our strategic growth priorities. Consolidated net 
capital expenditure during the first half of FY ‘27 was around INR 15 billion. 
This is broadly in line with our full year plans. What is particularly 
encouraging is the strength of a financial position despite this investment 
cycle. Through disciplined balance sheet management over the  past 
several years, we have significantly enhanced our financial flexibility. As on 
30th September 2026, consolidated net debt to EBITDA stood at just 0.9 
times, providing substantial capacity to  fund future growth while 
maintaining a prudent capital structure.  
 
Even under our ongoing investment plans, we are fairly confident that 
leverages will stay comfortably within our targeted range. This financial 
strength gives us the ability to continue investing in capacity expansion, 
digital transformation, technology, product development without 
compromising the balance sheet resilience. 
 
To conclude, while geopolitical developments and commodity volatility 
continue to create uncertainty in the near term, the underlying 
fundamentals of a business have continued to strengthen. Over the past 
several years, we have invested meaningfully in expanding and optimizing 
our manufacturing footprint, enhancing operational capabilities, 
strengthening our  brands, and building digital and technology platforms 
that improve decision-making and productivity across the organization. 
 
These investments are now increasingly translating into stronger 
competitive positioning and improved resilience across market cycles. We 
remain highly confident about the long- term opportunity ahead of us. In 
India, we are well positioned to benefit from a growing tyre market, 
improving product mix, and a more rational pricing strategy. In Europe, our 
focus remains on unlocking the full potential of  the business through 
improved competitiveness and a stronger operating model, enabling us 
leverage a significantly large profit pool. Supported by a healthy balance 
sheet, improved business fundamentals, and disciplined execution, we 
believe that Apollo Tyres is  well positioned to deliver sustainable growth 
and long-term value creation for our stakeholders.
